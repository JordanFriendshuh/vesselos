Vessel OS

Vessel OS is a personal operating system built around Claude. Instead of a desktop full of windows, you tell your computer what needs doing and it does the work, bringing you only the decisions that are yours to make.

Your calendar

When you connect your Google Calendar, Vessel shows your upcoming events on its Plans screen and puts the items you give a time onto your calendar, so the two stay in step. You connect it yourself, on your own computer, by pressing Allow on Google's own screen, and you can disconnect it at any time.
